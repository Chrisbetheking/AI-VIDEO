# Sound effect source

These WAV files were synthesized locally for AI-VIDEO using generated waveforms and envelopes.
No third-party audio binary is bundled.

Categories are designed for short-form editing cues:
- hook punch
- question ping
- turn whoosh
- data/list ticks
- risk warning
- camera click
- CTA ding
- soft pop

All files are mono PCM WAV at 48 kHz and are mixed under the narration with limiter and loudness normalization.
